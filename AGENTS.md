# AGENTS.md

## Public writing

Public prose in this repository follows the anti-AI-slop rules from:

https://github.com/realrossmanngroup/no_ai_slop_writing_rules

Use the anti-slop rules. Do not imitate Louis Rossmann's personal voice unless Rae explicitly asks for that.

Preserve Rae's meaning, wording, humor, uncertainty, and unfinished edges. Editing should make the writing plainer and more specific without making it sound professionally polished for its own sake.

Before committing public copy:

- remove em dashes;
- remove "not X, but Y" and "not just X" constructions unless the wording is clearly Rae's deliberate choice;
- remove filler, generic transitions, synthetic enthusiasm, and vague claims of importance;
- use descriptive headings instead of dramatic or teaser headings;
- prefer plain verbs over AI-favored verbs such as "leverage", "utilize", "foster", "underscore", and "delve";
- do not invent facts, dates, statistics, quotes, attributions, or project status;
- avoid repetitive section shapes and padded lists;
- keep concrete details when they exist;
- read the result once as ordinary human prose before committing it.
